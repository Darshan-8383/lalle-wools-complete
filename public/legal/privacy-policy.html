Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Who we are

LALLEWOOLS ("we", "us", "our") operates this website. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@lallewools.com.

2. Information we collect

When you place an order or create an account, we collect: your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address. If you pay online, payment is processed directly by our payment gateway (Razorpay) — we do not see or store your full card number, UPI PIN, or netbanking credentials.

3. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order, including sharing your name, address and phone number with our shipping partner to arrange delivery.
  • To send order confirmation, shipping, and delivery emails.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse of our checkout.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Who we share it with

  • Razorpay (payment processing) — receives your name, contact details, and order amount to process payment.
  • Shiprocket and the courier they assign (shipping) — receives your name, address, and phone number to deliver your order.
  • We do not share your data with anyone else except where required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain order records for as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes (typically several years, per Indian tax record-keeping requirements). You can request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us; note that we may need to retain order records even after account deletion for legal/accounting reasons.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing support@lallewools.com.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to keep your shopping bag and login session working between visits. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
