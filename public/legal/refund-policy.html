Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself, free of charge, any time before it has been shipped:

  • Log into your account → "My Orders" → Cancel, or
  • Contact us at support@lallewools.com with your order number.

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled — you're welcome to return it instead (see below) once delivered.

Refunds for cancelled orders

If you paid online and cancel before shipping, we automatically refund the full amount to your original payment method. Refunds are initiated immediately and typically reflect in your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Cash on Delivery orders that are cancelled before shipping simply don't get charged — there's nothing to refund.

Returns after delivery

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items in their original condition with tags attached. Contact us at support@lallewools.com with your order number to start a return; once we receive and inspect the item, we will process your refund to the original payment method (for Cash on Delivery orders, by bank transfer or UPI) within 7 business days.

Damaged or wrong item received

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging, and we'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

Non-returnable items

Custom/personalized items made to your specification are not eligible for return unless damaged or defective on arrival.

Questions

Email support@lallewools.com with your order number for anything not covered here.
