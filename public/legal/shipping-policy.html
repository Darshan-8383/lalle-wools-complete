Shipping Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Delivery timelines

We ship pan-India via our courier partners. At checkout, enter your PIN code to see an estimated delivery window for your area before you pay — this is checked live against our shipping partner's serviceability for your exact PIN code.

Typical delivery takes 3–7 business days from the date of dispatch depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer.

Shipping charges

A flat shipping charge of ₹50 applies to every order. Cash on Delivery orders also carry a ₹40 COD handling fee. Both are shown clearly at checkout before you confirm your order.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with your tracking/AWB number. You can also see live status any time by logging into your account and opening "My Orders".

Serviceability

While we aim to deliver across India, a small number of remote PIN codes may not be serviceable by our courier partners. If your PIN code isn't serviceable, you'll be notified at checkout before you pay.

Delays

Occasionally, deliveries may be delayed due to weather, courier operational issues, or events outside our control. We'll do our best to keep you informed if this happens to your order.

Questions

Email us at support@lallewools.com with your order number for any shipping questions.
